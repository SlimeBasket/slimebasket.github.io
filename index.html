Your island. Your crew. Your choices.

Build a captain’s legacy

A living island base

Gather wood, stone and iron, upgrade buildings through level 20 and design your combat defenses. Research, missions and daily objectives give your next voyage a purpose.

Aim, fire, bring it down

Use distinct projectile types, impact and collapsing structures to your advantage. Research monster ammunition on Fragment Island and choose the right shot for the target.

A talent tree of your own

Develop your captain across eight skill branches. Choose up to 21 normal talents, with additional selections available through mission skill points. Each branch leads toward its ultimate talent.

Explore dangerous waters

Discover resources, sea monsters and ghost ships. Your large ship can anchor at a destination and continue sailing from there. Repair damage at home or visit the harbour.

Islands with their own challenges

Face ten volcanic colony battles and 200 Spooky Island stages. Visit Fun Island for a risk-and-reward card mini-game with virtual prizes. There are no cash prizes or cash-out.

Rivals and fellow captains

Challenge other captains’ bases and ships, build your pirate reputation and send resource or booster gifts with preset pirate letters. Parrot Post brings gifts and battle reports back to your island.

Booty Island · Boss Alliance

Stand together against the Abyss Sovereign

A shared boss with 5,000 HP arrives each Friday at 07:00 and leaves on Sunday at 20:00, unless the captains defeat it first. Times follow Europe/Copenhagen, including daylight saving time.

Formations every 30 minutesChoose ammunition before joiningManual, waiting or crew combat

Visit Booty Island to see the enrolled captains and join a formation. More captains in that departure multiply projectile damage for that run. You can leave until one minute before departure. Let your crew fight while you do other things, or choose to wait for your own manual attack.

When the boss falls, every captain who dealt damage receives a 24-hour building-time booster. The top three contributors receive four each. No victory means no boss-defeat reward; Parrot Post delivers your contribution report.

Before you set sail

Captain’s essentials

Energy, repairs and boosters

Ordinary energy regenerates up to 20, with Energy Islands affecting regeneration. This regeneration adds energy directly, not Energy Packs. Ship damage persists between battles. Repair cost and time scale with level; Full Repair boosters repair the ship immediately without a resource cost.

Home waters and battles at sea

Monster attacks inside your home zone use your base. Outside the zone, your large ship sails to battle. Manual aiming rewards skill; Auto Battle offers a less precise alternative. Some events have their own rules and ammunition requirements.

The resource shop and advertisements

The current ammunition shop uses earned in-game resources and, for relevant monster ammunition, fragments. It is not a real-money checkout. The game contains advertising, including optional rewarded ads. Availability depends on the advertising service and your privacy choices. Virtual items cannot be redeemed for money.

Online features and saved progress

Connected features require an internet connection and supported sign-in. Google Play Games supports the Android identity flow. A hosted game server synchronizes connected progression, human player information, gifts and shared events. Some exploration and bot content remains local to the device. Existing Google/Firebase identity compatibility is retained; not every offline item is shared with other players.

Support & your privacy

Need help with a voyage, your account or a missing reward? Contact aarbyegames@gmail.com. Include your captain name and a description of the issue. Never send passwords or Google sign-in codes.

Read the privacy policy and account-deletion instructions. The game’s Privacy & Account menu provides privacy information, advertising choices, optional diagnostics and account deletion.

To request account deletion outside the game, email the support address with the subject Cannon Isle account deletion and enough information to identify your game account. Ownership may need to be verified. Deleting a game account does not delete your Google account.
