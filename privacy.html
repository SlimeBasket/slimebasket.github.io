Privacy policy for Cannon Isle
Updated 6 November 2026

Cannon Isle: Build & Battle is developed by aarbyegames. This policy explains information used when you play, sign in, use connected features or view ads.

Information used
Account and profile: Google Play Games identity, an opaque game account ID, your chosen player name and profile portrait. Existing accounts may retain their previous Firebase account ID so progression remains linked to the same player. Google or Firebase Authentication may process account information under their own policies; the game server does not store your Google password or Google sign-in codes in player profiles.

Game data: progress, resources, ammunition, unlocks, research, rewards, settings, base layouts, trophies, shields, selected portrait, pirate reputation, travel state, ship condition, battle reservations and results. Gifts include the sender and recipient, selected resources/packs and one of the game's preset letters. Skill progression includes selected talents, XP spending and skill-point rewards. Alliance boss events use enrollment, formation/run identifiers, selected ammunition, manual/crew/wait preferences, damage contributions, rankings and reward claims. Private player alliances store membership, roles, applications, resource contributions, funded upgrade tiers and crew messages. Crew chat is visible to members, filtered for basic prohibited content and links, and supports reporting and blocking. The server retains up to 80 recent crew messages for up to seven days. Moderation evidence can be retained separately for up to 90 days. Alliance names and basic recruitment information are visible to other captains. Leaving a crew does not refund contributions already donated.

Community safety data includes the accepted Pirate Code version, blocked captain IDs, reports of names/base designs/harassment, the relevant public content snapshot and moderation decisions. Moderation reports are retained for up to 90 days for review and abuse prevention.

Map data: resource mines, human player information, crew membership and boss events are synchronized with the game server. Depending on the installed version, bot state and ordinary sea collectibles may be local or synchronized with the server. Visual movement and full local replay histories remain on the device. Existing older backups can contain legacy local map data.

Advertising: Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform may process consent choices, device/app information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP and advertising identifiers when available and permitted. Ads are requested only when the consent platform permits them. Advertising privacy controls are available in the game's privacy menu. Current versions apply conservative under-age-of-consent ad treatment to all accounts because the game does not verify ages; this does not record or infer your age.

Optional diagnostics: Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics can process gameplay events, technical identifiers, device information and crash reports. Optional diagnostics are off by default and can be changed in the game's privacy menu.

How information is used
Information supports sign-in, saved progress, multiplayer, leaderboards, ship and base battles, rewards, security, advertising where permitted, and optional diagnostics. Network connections to the game server use HTTPS. Authentication/session credentials are used to protect access and are not published to other players. Server request counts, transferred data volumes and failed endpoint/status information are recorded for operation and diagnosis.

Public game information
Other players can see your chosen name, profile portrait, level, trophies, pirate reputation, published island/base location, mine ownership and the visible status/routes of your large ship, including its selected ship skin. These are game-world coordinates, not your real-world location. Gift recipients can see the sender's name and portrait. Alliance participants can see enrolled captains' names, portraits and levels. Shared boss status and the leading contributors' names and damage can be visible to other participants. Do not include personal or sensitive information in your player name or crew messages.

Service providers
The game uses an aarbyegames-operated server hosted by IDhosting for connected game data. Google Play Games supports sign-in/platform features; Firebase Authentication supports existing account compatibility. Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform provide advertising and consent. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics support optional diagnostics. Legacy Firebase game storage has been replaced by the hosted game server in current versions. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Retention and choices
Local information remains until app data is cleared or the game is uninstalled. Connected account and gameplay data is retained while your account is active or as needed to operate the service. Security records and backups may be retained as necessary for security and applicable obligations; provider retention policies may differ. You can decline online sign-in and use the available privacy/consent controls.

Delete your account and data
In the game, open the privacy screen, choose Delete Account and confirm. This removes the connected game account and its associated account/gameplay data. It does not delete your Google account. Local offline data remains until you clear app data or uninstall the game.

For deletion outside the game, email aarbyegames@gmail.com with the subject Cannon Isle account deletion. Include your player display name and enough information to identify the account. We may verify ownership. Never send passwords or sign-in codes. For questions about your personal data or these deletion instructions, contact this address.
